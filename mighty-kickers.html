MIGHTY KICKERS

Bigger skills. Bigger confidence.

Mighty Kickers introduces young players to more structured football activities while keeping every session fun.

Children begin to develop stronger football techniques while learning how to work together.

DEVELOPING SKILLS

More football. More fun.

Sessions introduce children to increasingly challenging football activities while encouraging creativity and confidence.

Players are encouraged to make decisions, interact with teammates and experiment with their football skills.

  • ⚽ Dribbling and ball control
  • ⚽ Passing and movement
  • ⚽ Fun team games
  • ⚽ Confidence and communication
INSIDE A SESSION

Ready, set, kick!

Every session combines football skills with imaginative games and challenges.

01

Warm Up

Skills

Challenges

Match Play

DEVELOPING YOUNG PLAYERS

Skills that go beyond football.

Football provides children with a fun environment to practise physical, communication and social skills.

Ball Skills Developing greater control and coordination.
Teamwork Learning to cooperate with other players.
Decision Making Encouraging players to think and react.

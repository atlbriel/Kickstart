JUNIOR KICKERS

Growing skills. Growing confidence.

Junior Kickers gives young players the chance to become more comfortable with the football and their surroundings.

Sessions introduce more structured games while keeping the focus firmly on fun and enjoyment.

LEARN THROUGH PLAY

Building the basics.

Children begin developing a stronger understanding of the ball while learning simple football movements.

Activities are designed to encourage independence, listening and interaction with other young players.

  • ⚽ Ball control activities
  • ⚽ Movement and coordination
  • ⚽ Simple football games
  • ⚽ Confidence and independence

DEVELOPING YOUNG PLAYERS

Every kick is a new skill.

Junior players are encouraged to explore football at their own pace while developing important physical and social skills.

Coordination Developing control, balance and movement.
Listening Following simple instructions through games.
Confidence Encouraging children to try new things.

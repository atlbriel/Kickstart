MEGA KICKERS

Bigger games. Bigger challenges.

Mega Kickers gives young players the opportunity to take their football skills to the next level.

Sessions combine technical development, challenges and team games in a fun environment.

LEVEL UP

Ready for the next step?

Players are encouraged to develop their technique while becoming more confident playing with others.

Sessions introduce more challenging football activities without losing the fun that makes young players want to keep coming back.

  • ⚽ Dribbling and close control
  • ⚽ Passing and receiving
  • ⚽ Shooting and finishing
  • ⚽ Team games and challenges
INSIDE A SESSION

Football with a challenge.

Players get the chance to practise their skills and put them into action.

01

Warm Up

Get moving with football-focused activities and games.

02

Technique

Develop control, passing, movement and finishing.

03

Challenge

Put individual skills to the test through exciting games.

04

Team Play

Work together and enjoy competitive football activities.

MORE THAN FOOTBALL

Building players for the future.

As children grow, football can help them develop physical skills, confidence, communication and decision-making.

Technique Developing control and football fundamentals.
Teamwork Learning how to communicate and cooperate.
Confidence Encouraging players to take on challenges.

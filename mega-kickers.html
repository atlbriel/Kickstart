MEGA KICKERS

Bigger games. Bigger challenges.

Mega Kickers gives young players the opportunity to take their football skills to the next level.

Sessions combine technical development, challenges and team games in a fun environment.

LEVEL UP

Ready for the next step?

Players are encouraged to develop their technique while becoming more confident playing with others.

Sessions introduce more challenging football activities without losing the fun that makes young players want to keep coming back.

  • ⚽ Dribbling and close control
  • ⚽ Passing and receiving
  • ⚽ Shooting and finishing
  • ⚽ Team games and challenges

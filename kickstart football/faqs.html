Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents most often ask us.

Do parents need to participate?

For our youngest classes(18 months-3 years),a parents or a caregiver joins in the fun to support thei child. As children grow in confidence and independence, they participate on their own while parents watch and cheer from the sidelines.

What should my child wear to class?

Comfortable sports clothing, runners and don't forget a water bottle. Have you seen our KickStart jersey and shorts set is available for just Ksh 2,500, perfect for training and game days!

What age group do you cater to?

We offer classes for children from 18 months to 10years, with sessions tailored to each developmental stage:

- 18 months- 3 years: Parent & Child

- 3-5 years: Preschool

- 5-7 years: Early School Age

- 7-10 years: Skills & Game Play

What happens in a typical Kickstart class?

Every class is packed with fun! Children enjoy age appropriate games, ball skills, movement activites and teamwork challenges in a postive, encouraging environment. Our focus is on building confidence, coordination, friendships and a lifelong love of football.

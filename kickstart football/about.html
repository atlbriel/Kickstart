About KickStart Football

KickStart Football is a playful and nurturing soccer program based in Nairobi, Kenya, designed especially for children aged 18 months to 10 years.

Our weekly sessions blend the joy of movement with the fundamentals of football, creating a safe and supportive space where children build confidence, coordination, teamwork and a lifelong love for the game.

Grounded in child-centered coaching and play, our sessions go beyond basic drills. We focus on core motor skills like balance, coordination, spatial awareness and agility — the foundation for all physical activity and brain-body connection.

As children progress, they also develop teamwork, emotional regulation, focus and confidence, all within a safe and encouraging environment.

ABOUT US

Football. Fun. Confidence.

Creating positive football experiences for children and families.

OUR STORY

More than just football.

We believe football should be fun, welcoming and accessible to every child.

Our sessions give children the opportunity to learn, play and develop while enjoying themselves in a positive environment.

OUR IMPACT

Making a difference through football.

Every session is an opportunity to create positive experiences, build confidence and bring children and families together.

1,200+ Children Reached

Children have taken part in our football activities and programmes.

200+ Households Supported

Families and households connected with our programmes and community activities.

WHAT WE DO

Helping children fall in love with football.

Our programmes are designed around age-appropriate activities that help children become comfortable with movement, football and teamwork.

From their first kick to more advanced football activities, we want every child to leave a session feeling proud of what they have achieved.
